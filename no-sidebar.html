Founder

Rahul Singh

Building Neo Qāsi as a founder-led practice for advisory, research, and digital execution.

Neo Qāsi is led by Rahul Singh, a consultant working at the intersection of public systems, social impact, data, and digital transformation. The practice is being shaped to offer a more coherent front door for assignments that require strategic thinking, analytical depth, and practical execution.

Rather than positioning itself as a generic agency, Neo Qāsi is intentionally founder-led. That matters because many clients are not looking for scale theatre; they are looking for judgment, clarity, structured outputs, and someone who understands how real institutions work.

Why assignments can come through Neo Qāsi

The strongest reason is simple: the work already exists. Founder experience spans multilateral assignments, government-facing consulting, digital strategy, proposal development, programme design, analytical research, and systems thinking. Neo Qāsi gives that work a clearer identity and a more focused delivery proposition.

How Neo Qāsi works

Neo Qāsi is built around a simple operating idea: combine clear thinking with practical delivery. That means understanding the institutional context, framing the problem properly, and producing work that is usable by decision-makers, not just impressive on paper.

The practice is suitable for direct assignments, consulting partnerships, proposal support roles, and collaboration with larger firms or consortiums that need high-quality thinking and founder-led execution.
