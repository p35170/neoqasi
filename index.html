Why this matters

For Neo Qāsi, publications are not ornamental content. They reflect the same analytical discipline we bring to consulting work: clear framing, structured reasoning, policy awareness, and practical relevance.

They also help potential clients understand how we think before we ever get into a proposal, a scope note, or an assignment conversation.

Why this practice is credible

Neo Qāsi is led by Rahul Singh, whose work spans multilateral organizations, government-facing consulting, digital transformation assignments, and evidence-led programme support. Founder experience includes assignments linked to UNICEF, UNDP, UNOICT/UNOV, UNODC, NITI WEP, the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, NHAI-related research, and the Government of Uttar Pradesh.

That background brings together four strengths that are rarely combined in one practice: policy thinking, analytical depth, programme design, and digital execution.
