Gyaan

Advisory, research, and programme support

For assignments that need substance, structure, and institutional understanding.

Gyaan is the advisory arm of Neo Qāsi. It is designed for assignments where the client does not just need desk research or report writing, but a partner who can understand the policy context, clarify the problem, shape the response, and deliver decision-useful outputs.

What we do

Programme and policy design. We support the early architecture of initiatives through scoping studies, concept notes, programme logic, implementation pathways, and budget-linked thinking.

Evidence and analytical support. We work with public datasets, sectoral evidence, expenditure logic, and issue-based research to develop briefs, notes, and insights that decision-makers can actually use.

Proposal and methodology development. We help shape the technical backbone of bids, donor proposals, and partnership documents with sharper framing, stronger logic, and clearer execution pathways.

Institutional and sector research. We undertake structured research for governance, infrastructure, social justice, inclusive growth, disaster resilience, and programme delivery questions.
